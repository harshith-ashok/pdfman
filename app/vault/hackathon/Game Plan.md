# Game Plan  
  
Frontend - Vue  
Backend - PHP  
DB - Mongodb  
  
Many citizens face legal disputes but cannot afford lawyers or easily access professional legal support.  
Although courts allow individuals to represent themselves, legal procedures are complex, document-  
heavy, and difficult to understand.  
People often receive legal notices or court documents but struggle to understand:  
• what the document means,  
• what action is required,  
• what to file next,  
• when deadlines occur,  
• how to prepare for hearings.  
Legal information is fragmented, written in technical language, and often available only in English.  
Missing procedural steps or deadlines can negatively impact cases even when citizens have valid claims.  
Access to justice requires tools that help citizens understand procedures clearly and confidently without  
replacing legal professionals.
